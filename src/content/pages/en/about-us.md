## Our story

It began with our own travels around Southeast Asia and with friends asking us to plan theirs. In October 2023 we registered Công ty TNHH Blue Starfish Travel Boutique in Ho Chi Minh City, a tour operator focused on travel in Vietnam. In 2025 a second partner joined, bringing thirty years of building brands across Europe and Asia, much of it in Vietnam.

Since then we have planned trips for more than thirty groups, and all 32 of our Google reviews are five stars. We stay boutique on purpose: every route is built by someone who knows you from the conversations before departure and answers the phone during the trip.
