## Our story

It started with our own travels around Southeast Asia and with friends asking where to stay, what to see and how to get there. In October 2023 Adam registered Công ty TNHH Blue Starfish Travel Boutique in Ho Chi Minh City, a licensed operator of inbound travel to Vietnam. Two years later Andrzej joined as co-owner, after years of living in Vietnam and travelling with our guests.

We are a small company and intend to stay one. Each trip is planned by one person who knows you from the conversations before departure and answers the phone during the trip.

## How we work

**We listen before we propose.** We start with a conversation: how much time you have, who you travel with, what you want to avoid. Only then do we draw the route.

**Everything in one place.** Hotels, domestic flights, drivers, guides, tickets. You get one plan and one phone number.

**We are on the ground.** We live in Ho Chi Minh City, so we check hotels in person, know the prices and know when it rains.

**Honest prices.** A small company has small overheads. We quote one price for the whole trip, with nothing to pay on arrival.

**We support local people.** We work with family-run hotels, local guides and small restaurants, not chains.
