## Who controls your data

Công ty TNHH Blue Starfish Travel Boutique, 94 Xuân Thủy, Phường Thảo Điền, Thành phố Thủ Đức, Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam (tax ID 0318118010). Data questions: contact@bluestarfishtravel.com.

## What we collect and why

The form on this site collects your name, email address, optionally a phone number, and details of the trip you are planning (destination, dates, length, who is travelling, budget, notes). We use them only to answer your question and prepare a trip proposal. The legal basis is taking steps at your request before entering into a contract (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)).

If you write to us on WhatsApp or by email, we process what you send for the same purpose.

## Who processes data on our behalf

- EmailJS (EmailJS Ltd.) delivers the form to our mailbox.
- Google Analytics measures site traffic in aggregate. We do not link it to form contents.
- Google Maps shows the map on the About page; loading it sends your IP address to Google.

## How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to a trip are deleted after 12 months. Records of guests who travelled with us are kept as long as tax law requires.

## Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, a correction or deletion by writing to contact@bluestarfishtravel.com. You may also complain to a supervisory authority; in the EU, the authority of the country where you live.

## Cookies

The site uses Google Analytics cookies to measure traffic. You can block them in your browser; the site works the same without them.
